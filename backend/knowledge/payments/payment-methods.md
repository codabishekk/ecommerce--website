# Payment Information

## Accepted payment methods
Skin Care Product accepts the following payment methods:
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express)
- Debit cards
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)
- Net banking (all major Indian banks)
- Cash on Delivery (COD)

## Cash on Delivery
Cash on Delivery is available on all orders across India. There is no additional charge for COD orders. COD orders are subject to address verification; in some cases the order may be cancelled if verification fails.

## Payment security
All online payments are processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. Skin Care Product does not store card details on its servers.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, no amount is deducted from the customer's account. If an amount was deducted but the order was not placed, the amount is automatically reversed by the bank within 5-7 working days. Contact support if the reversal does not happen.

## Payment confirmation
Once a payment is successful, customers receive an order confirmation email with the order details.

## Order amount confirmation
The total amount charged is the amount shown at checkout, including any discounts and excluding shipping (shipping is free).
