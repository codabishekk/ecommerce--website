# Customer Support Information

## How to reach us
- AI support assistant: available 24/7 on our website
- Email: support@sholashlifescience.com
- Phone: 9500522551

## Support hours
Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST. Queries received outside these hours are answered on the next working day.

## What our support team can help with
- Order status and delivery questions
- Returns, refunds, and cancellations
- Product questions and ingredient information
- Payment issues
- Damaged or incorrect orders
- Account help

## Escalation to human support
If the AI assistant cannot resolve your issue, or if you explicitly ask to speak with an agent, a support ticket is created and a human agent will contact you. Keep your order number ready to speed up the process.

## Response time
Support tickets are responded to within one working day. Urgent issues, such as damaged deliveries, are prioritised.
