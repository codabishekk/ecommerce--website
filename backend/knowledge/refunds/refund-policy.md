# Refund Policy

## When do refunds apply?
Refunds apply when an order is cancelled before dispatch, or when an approved return has been received and inspected, or when a product arrives damaged or incorrect.

## Refund timelines
Refunds are processed within 5-7 working days of approval. After processing, the time taken for the amount to reflect in the customer's account depends on the payment method and bank:
- Credit/debit card: 5-7 working days
- UPI and net banking: 3-5 working days
- Cash on Delivery: refunds are issued via UPI or bank transfer once the customer provides their details

## Refund method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout. For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are processed to the customer's UPI ID or bank account after verification.

## Order cancellation before dispatch
If an order is cancelled before it is dispatched, the full amount is refunded. There are no cancellation charges.

## Failed refunds
If a refund fails or is delayed beyond the expected timeline, customers can raise the issue with our support team. The support team will investigate and re-process the refund if needed.

## Partial refunds
In cases where only part of an order is returned, or products are returned in a non-resaleable condition, a partial refund may be issued at the discretion of the support team.
