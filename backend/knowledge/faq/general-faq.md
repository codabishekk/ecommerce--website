# General FAQ

## What is Skin Care Product?
Skin Care Product is an e-commerce skincare brand. We formulate and sell dermatologist-inspired skincare products in India. Products are available for purchase directly through our website.

## Are Skin Care products tested on animals?
No. Skin Care Product does not test its products on animals. Our formulations are developed and tested under controlled laboratory conditions.

## Are the products suitable for sensitive skin?
Many of our products are formulated to be gentle and suitable for sensitive skin. However, every skin is different. We recommend doing a patch test before first use, and if you have a known skin condition, consult a dermatologist before starting any new skincare routine.

## Where can I see the full ingredients list for a product?
Complete ingredient information is available on each product's detail page on our website, under the ingredients section. If you have specific questions about an ingredient, contact our support team.

## How do I track my order?
You can track your order status by asking our AI support assistant, by logging into your account profile, or by contacting our customer support team with your order number.
