# Cancellation Policy

## When can I cancel?
Orders can be cancelled only while they are in the 'processing' status, that is, before they are dispatched. Once an order has moved to 'shipped' or 'delivered', it cannot be cancelled and must instead go through the return process if eligible.

## How to cancel
You can cancel an eligible order through the AI support assistant. The assistant will confirm the order details and your intent before cancelling. Cancellation can also be requested by contacting customer support.

## Confirmation requirement
For security, cancellation is only performed after you explicitly confirm the action. A confirmation link is generated and must be confirmed by the authenticated account owner.

## Cancellation charges
There are no cancellation charges. The full amount paid is refunded to the original payment method.

## Refund after cancellation
If the order was paid online, the refund is processed within 5-7 working days of cancellation. For Cash on Delivery orders cancelled before dispatch, no payment was collected, so nothing is refunded.

## Partial cancellation
Cancelling only part of an order is not supported. Cancellation applies to the full order. If you need to reduce quantities, contact human support.

## Failed dispatch cancellation
If a carrier cannot deliver the order and it is returned to us, the order will be cancelled and refunded automatically.
