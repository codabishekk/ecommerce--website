# Return Policy

## Return window
You may request a return within 7 days of the delivery date. Returns are only accepted for orders that have been marked as delivered.

## Condition of returned products
Returned products must be unused, unopened, and in their original packaging. Products that show signs of use beyond a reasonable inspection, or that have damaged packaging caused by the customer, may not be eligible for a full return.

## Non-returnable items
Items that have been opened and used (beyond reasonable inspection), products past their return window, and products returned without original packaging are not eligible for return.

## Damaged or incorrect items
If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos. We will arrange a replacement or a full refund, including any applicable shipping charges.

## How to request a return
You can request a return through our AI support assistant or by contacting customer support with your order number. A return request will be created and reviewed by our team. Approved returns receive instructions on how to send the product back.

## Return pickup
Pickup for approved returns is arranged by our logistics partners. A reverse pickup slot is scheduled at a convenient time for the customer.

## Refund after return
Once the returned product is received and inspected, the refund is processed within 5-7 working days. The refund amount is credited back to the original payment method.
