# Shipping Policy

## Delivery areas
Sholash Life Science currently ships to all pin codes across India. We do not ship internationally at this time.

## Processing time
Orders are processed and dispatched within 1-2 working days after the order is placed. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are dispatched on the next working day.

## Delivery time
Standard delivery takes 3-7 working days depending on the delivery location. Metro cities typically receive orders within 2-4 working days. Remote areas may take up to 7 working days.

## Shipping charges
Shipping is FREE on all orders across India. There are no hidden delivery charges.

## Order tracking
Once an order is dispatched, customers receive an email confirmation. Order status can be checked at any time through the AI support assistant or the customer support team.

## Delivery partners
We use trusted logistics partners including India Post, Delhivery, and other courier services depending on the delivery address.

## Delayed deliveries
In rare cases, deliveries may be delayed due to courier partner or weather conditions. If an order is delayed beyond the expected delivery window, contact support and we will investigate immediately.
